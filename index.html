Date Calculator

Any math with dates with and keep it simple as possible

Dates
25.12.2026, 2026-12-25, 12/25/2026, 22 Nov 1996, today, next Friday, следующую пятницу, щас. A date that stops at the month is the first day of it: November 1996, ноябрь 1996, 11.1996, 2026
Amounts
10 days, 33y, 1ч30м, -4 weeks 4 days, 3 сек
Ranges
22.11.1996 - 25.11.1996, today - 29.08.2026 — every day in between; a picked run of four days or more is written back this way
Between
a second date instead of an amount gives the gap between the two
Repeats
* 2 days, every week, каждые 3 дня — every day in the first field moves on by that step. A range moves as a block instead: 22.11.1996 - 25.11.1996 with * 4 d is four days on, four days off
Days off
the ( ) button next to the grid puts brackets on a repeat: * week (04.05.2026 - 10.05.2026). Days picked while it is on go inside them and drop out of the repeat, and a turn with nothing left in it is skipped whole
Calendar
click fills the first field, ctrl-click the second, shift-click or a long press picks several days. The month title is a field of its own, and it reads whatever the first field reads — November 1996, ноябрь 1996, 11.1996, next year — the grid opens there instead of on today
Keys
Enter calculates, Tab swaps fields, Esc steps out of them; on desktop just start typing anywhere
Answers
share one as a link, or send a single date to Google Calendar — a repeat travels with it
